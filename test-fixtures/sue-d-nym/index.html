About Sue

I spent eleven years as a VP of Operations before I became a coach — which means I've sat in the room when a leader avoided the real conversation for six months and watched what it cost the team. I don't do vague affirmations and I don't do corporate-speak. I tell people what I actually see, and then I help them fix it before their next meeting, not their next quarter.

My clients are usually first-time executives, or long-tenured experts who just got promoted into managing people for the first time. They're good at the work. The work was never the problem. The problem is the conversation they've been avoiding.

Services

The Hard Conversation Clinic

A single, intensive session built around one specific conversation you're dreading — a layoff, a performance issue, a boundary you need to set with your own boss. We script it, stress-test it, and you leave with exact language, not just a feeling of readiness.

Single session — 90 minutes

Executive Presence Intensive

An 8-week 1:1 program for leaders who are technically excellent but get overlooked in the room — talked over, not promoted, not taken seriously in negotiations. We work on voice, framing, and the specific habits that are quietly costing you influence.

8 weeks — weekly sessions

New Leader Onboarding Sprint

A 90-day program for first-time managers and executives, built around the three conversations every new leader gets wrong in month one: setting expectations, addressing the underperformer they inherited, and establishing credibility with a team that didn't choose them.

90 days — biweekly sessions + on-call text support
